Plot Summary
- The party is sent to deal with a problem that should be instantly familiar to all rookie adventurers - exterminating alehouse imps that have taken up residence in an ale cellar. However, if they are to achieve their goal our heroes are going to have to get their first taste of a real dungeon as they explore the ruins of an ancient wizard’s tower.
Set-Up
- [[Wizard's Tower Brewing Co.]]
	- Craft brewery known for hoppy summer ales
	- Party accepts job via notice board
		- Meet [[Glowkindle]] at WTB
		- Innkeepers/drunks happy to point the way if asked politely
Meeting [[Glowkindle]]
- [[Glowkindle]]
	- Elderly, impressively bearded gnome with a white shirt, red buttons, and blue pants
- Startled before recognizing party as adventurers and offers drinks and job
	- Imps got into cellar during business expansion
		- Hired a traveling cleric to bless the new project
			- Cleric got drunk and flubbed incantation
				- Accidentally summoned mephits
				- Cleric shunted to other plane
	- Party needs to deal with the mephits in the cellar for 25g/person reward
Ale Cellar (1)
- The wooden stairs creak as you descend into the cool, dry air of the cellar, which is infused with the smell of beer and faint sulfur. Somewhere in the darkness you hear the light sound of flapping wings and high-pitched voices.
- Darkvision or lit torches allow visibility of the hole (4ft. sphere)  in the wall where the cleric’s spell opened a gateway to [[Carnheim]].
- DC10 Perception
	- Notice Ale Mephits drinking from a barrel
