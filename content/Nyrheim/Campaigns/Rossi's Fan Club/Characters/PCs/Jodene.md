#Backstory
Kidnapped by fey as a young child, Jodene (Jo) is determined to find her way from [[Litheim]] back to her human family one way or the other. She's young, naive and isn't used to [[Nyrheim]], which causes her problems in day-to-day interactions. 
	--Jo is unaware (player is not) that she was actually traded to her adoptive fey parents in exchange for pact magic. Her biological parents gave her up.--
#Background
*Feytouched
- Feature: Kindred Spirit
	- Even though you may not be of actual Fey blood or racial stock all Fey you encounter will recognize you as Feytouched and treat you as one of their own. You know the customs and culture of Fey society and find acceptance in their communities.
	- Feytouched that leave the Feywild will automatically succeed on any saving throws needed to retain their memories of their stay in the Feywild but such memories can be jumbled or a little hazy and contribute to their overall eccentric behaviors.
- Personality Traits
	- I misquote and misunderstand normal sayings and idioms in almost every situation.
	- I’ve spent so long in the fay world at I have little practical experience dealing with people in the other world.
- Ideals
	- Charity. I always try to help those in need, no matter what the personal cost. (Good)
- Bonds
	- I will someday get revenge on the wild fey who kidnapped me as a child and tried to brainwash me into forgetting who my family was.
- Flaws
	- Once I pick a goal, I become obsessed with it to the detriment of everything else in my life.
	- I am suspicious of strangers and expect they're fey trying to spirit me back.
