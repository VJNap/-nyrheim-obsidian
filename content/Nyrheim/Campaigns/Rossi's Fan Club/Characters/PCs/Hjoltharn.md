#Backstory
As a child, Hjoltharn was raised in Bar Ka'het to a family of devout followers of the dark god [[Dolor]]. Corporal punishment was a way of life for young Hjoltharn, not only because of the teachings of [[Dolor]], but because he was apt to cause mischief; often leading to him being beaten bloody by his parents. This emotionally scarred Hjoltharn, who pledged to be better than his parents, for fear of becoming as callous and brutish as them.
	
He ran away from home at age 16. While still young by human and Minotaur standards, he was already well over 6'6" and made money in local fighting rings to get by. As he got older, and larger, his work changed from fighting for sport to protection of local merchants. He did this for several years, even garnering a successful reputation in Bar Ka'het, until he was hired to guard a human diplomat who he failed to protect.
	
Disgraced, he left the city of [[Bar Ka'het]] and wandered the plains of central [[Highgard]] where he happened upon an old dwarven merchant beset upon by a duo of leonin. As the merchant spots you, he tries to turn away, but the leonin catch up with the dwarf and knock him off his horse. Despite his better judgment, Hjoltharn charges the leonin and after a hard-fought battle, manages to protect the merchant. The merchant introduces himself as [[Barthrum Tufden]] who offers Hjoltharn a job as his personal bodyguard which he accepts. The pair set across the land developing a bond more reminiscent of father/son than employer/employee. It was through Barthrum that Hjoltharn learned leatherworking, which gave him an outlet to create as opposed to destroy, as his upbringing would suggest. 
	
Their time together was cut short as Barthrum succumbed to age, but Hjoltharn continues to work on his leatherworking and mercantile skills in hopes of becoming one of the region's most successful leathercrafters and leather armorsmiths. He now lives in a cottage on the banks of the [[Nodam River]] just west of [[King’s Harbor]], working on his craft and his need to be more than "just a brutish minotaur," but he thinks of and longs for one last adventure.

*Physical Description
- Large, even by Minotaur standards, Hjoltharn is immensely strong and it shows. With a massive neck, broad shoulders, and imposing horns, he stands head and shoulders above most Centaurs and Leonin and absolutely towers above humans. His mane of dark red hair only adds to his immense appearance. 
- He has forward-swept horns with notches in them, symbolizing the enemies he's killed or defeated in combat, and has similar markings tattooed on his arm for the people he's protected in his time as a bodyguard.
- His fur is reminiscent in color and pattern of Hereford cattle and covers pitch-black skin.
