#Backstory
- Born: [[Cairnton]], [[Highgard]]
- Hometown: [[Rivrun]], [[Highgard]]
- Fought in: [[Battle of Griffin Valley]] - [[Dread Gulf Incursion]]
“Corporal Kashiya of the [[32nd Skirmisher Battalion|32nd Sticks]].” is how most people are greeted by the tall, bald human, usually while leaning on his halberd. Born 25 year prior, his mother was a prostitute and his father was a particularly virile forgotten john. His memories of his mother are happy ones, with songs and play. When he was 6, though, she just never returned home one day. Forced to grow up faster than he, or his mother, had intended, Kashiya took a myriad of work, from manning cotton looms to threshing hay on the farms. Though the scent of lavender still has him look around, in case his mother finally found him after all these years.

The years went by, the physical labor molding Kashiya into a strong young man, but was otherwise unremarkable. One of the farmer’s wives insisted on teaching him basic reading and numbers, no matter how much her husband hemed on about the harvest. For a couple of years he worked with a goblin crew and was able to pick up the bones of the language. The real change came when a regional conflict broke out and the military called for soldiers. Kashiya enlisted and was assigned to the [[32nd Skirmisher Battalion]], better known as the [[32nd Skirmisher Battalion|32nd Sticks]] for the halberds that they carried. Kashiya was a quick learner and made friends quickly, and counted [[Kato ]]as his best friend. He felt he had finally found his true home and calling in life.

Basically trained and commanded by [[Sir Arthur Dinsmoor]] the battalion met the larger army and marched for what would be the decisive battle. Assigned the place of honor, the [[32nd Skirmisher Battalion|32nd]] was positioned on the far right of the army, to shore up its flanks. When the battle commenced, the [[32nd Skirmisher Battalion|32nd]] held its own admirably, Kashiya himself able to unhorse and slay a cavalryman. However, the battalion was out maneuvered, and while pinned by one cavalry group, another smashed into their flank. The last thing Kashiya remembered was the flash of a horse's chest hitting him in the head at charging speed.
		 
Waking up, a disoriented and concussed Kashiya realized that he was trapped under the bodies of his comrades. Able to barely free his head, he witnessed the remainder of the [[32nd Skirmisher Battalion|32nd]], including [[Sir Arthur Dinsmoor|Sir Dinsmoor]] lined up and executed under the orders of the cavalry regiment commander, [[Sir Villiam Vach]]. By the time he was able to free himself, the regiment had moved on and he was all alone.
	
Taking a halberd, he left the campaign. He felt he was a failure, that if he had fought harder, angle his weapon differently, and was strong enough to get out from under the pile of bodies, some of his friends would still be alive. Occasionally, when he turns a corner, he sees a fleeting image of [[Kato]]. He doesn't know if it's a ghost or from the knock on his head, but either way, he can feel the judgment of his comrade. 
	
Taking odd guard jobs here and there, Kashiya is resolved for two goals. Firstly, reform the [[32nd Skirmisher Battalion|32nd Sticks]], either as a mercenary company or in an official army. Second, hunt down and exact revenge on [[Sir Villiam Vach|Vach]] and the 4 battalion commanders of the cavalry regiment that killed his friends, first in battle and then in cold blood.
